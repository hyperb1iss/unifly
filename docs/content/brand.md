The Circuit Dragonfly is unifly's mark: four angular wings around a central
spine, in SilkCircuit purple and cyan. Use the flat artwork when linking to
the project, writing about it, or making something for the community.

![The unifly repository card](../brand/repo-card.png)

## Keep it clean

Preserve the wing shapes, their spacing, and the mark's proportions. Leave
clear space around the artwork (at least the width of the central spine on
each side). Use the light-background variants on pale surfaces, or the
single-color marks when color is unavailable.

The primary palette is electric purple `#e135ff`, cyan `#80ffea`, off-white
`#f8f8f2`, and dark violet `#12101a`. Light-background variants use purple
`#7e2bd5`, teal `#006e72`, and ink `#2b2540`.

Keep the mark flat. Avoid gradients, glow, shadows, bevels, textures, or
distortion. Keep the logo wordmark lowercase: **unifly**.
